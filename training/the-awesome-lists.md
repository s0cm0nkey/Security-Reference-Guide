# The Awesome Lists

Awesome lists are community-curated indexes that collect tools, writeups, papers, labs, and references around a topic. They are great for discovery, but they vary widely in freshness and quality.

This page is the broad Awesome-list registry for the guide. Awesome lists are useful enough that they may also appear on relevant section pages when they help the reader in context.

Use these search tools when you want to explore beyond the cherry-picked lists below:

* [GitHub Awesome topic](https://github.com/topics/awesome)
* [Awesome Indexed](https://awesome-indexed.mathew-davies.co.uk/)
* [Awesome Search](https://awesomelists.top/)
* [cheat.sh](https://github.com/chubin/cheat.sh) - Command-line friendly cheatsheet and reference lookup.

{% hint style="info" %}
This list is not comprehensive. It is intentionally limited to collections that are useful to security practitioners.
{% endhint %}
